The [4 November functional update](docs/GAP-CLOSURE-2026-11-04.md) adds scoped independent operator review, buyer-confirmed time-policy checks, a configurable JavaScript-form adapter and separately recorded complete AI regression/transfer validation. Agree on the scope, let the seller submit evidence, and let the buyer accept the exact delivery independently of payment.

Built on BriefPay as a learning project for the [PayPal AI Hackathon](https://paypalaihackathon.devpost.com/). The website-first workflow combines requirements extraction with delivery checking: type website requirements directly or optionally add agreement text/TXT, edit and confirm the checklist as buyer, submit a bounded HTML/CSS website folder with local PNG/JPEG/WebP photos as seller, then inspect the exact delivery before accepting it independently of payment. Only newly created built-in demonstrations carry the server-recorded `demoSampleProject` flag that permits fixed sample files. Custom and older unmarked role-play projects keep uploads and existing history but cannot save new fixed samples. Separate authenticated buyer/seller collaboration, an explicit two-account Sandbox testing choice and the earlier single-user PayPal Sandbox tool remain selectable. It supports Chinese and English. Existing BriefPay quotes remain stored and keep their original checkout workflow; they are not silently converted into proof agreements.

## Verify a booking promise beyond its success message

The optional buyer-confirmed **booking HTTP protocol** adds a bounded backend check to an included original requirement. The original `booking-http-v1` protocol retains its eight steps: revision, structured input rejection, creation, independent readback, idempotent replay, conflicting input and an unchanged final record. Existing v1 plans and reports are not silently upgraded. The seller declares a compatible operator-registered disposable target. Reports retain the exact uploaded-file SHA-256 digest, plan, target and delivery. They do not attest that a separately deployed backend was built from those uploaded files. Failed or unavailable steps require explicit buyer acknowledgement and an exception reason before acceptance; later reports do not rewrite an earlier acceptance's evidence. See [the basic protocol and historical hosted observations](docs/BOOKING-HTTP-VERIFICATION.md).

The new **eighteen-step business protocol**, `booking-http-v2`, derives a single maximum party size from a clear included original clause, for example “每笔预约最多6人” or “Each booking accepts at most 6 people.” The supported maximum is an integer from 2–12; prices, minimums, ambiguous wording and conflicting limits remain unsupported. The buyer separately confirms this policy and technical agreement. The run checks one guest, zero and fractional inputs, `maximum−1`, the exact maximum, `maximum+1`, independent boundary readback and two concurrent same-key requests. A local native-SQLite integration checkpoint demonstrates a backend that passes all eight basic checks yet actually stores seven guests against an agreed maximum of six: 17 of 18 steps have support and the upper-bound step mismatches. The corrected six-person backend supports all 18 at that checkpoint. Those initial results were in-process runner/handler/SQLite checks. A later controlled hosted seller-reference run separately retained the native-seven mismatch (17/18) and corrected native-six result (18/18), as documented in the [dated iteration record](docs/WINNER-ITERATIONS-2026-11-02.md#round-3-inspectable-evidence-and-a-native-policy-reference-deployment). That historical run used developer-operated synthetic roles and separate seller tables in the lab’s shared physical D1 database; it was not an independently operated third-party seller. The same fixed backend is also checked against buyer limits of five and seven to show that expectations come from the original requirement. Read [the business policy, recovery semantics and evidence budget](docs/BOOKING-POLICY-CHECKS.md).

The optional **seventeen-step capacity protocol**, `booking-http-v3`, requires two separately included original clauses: a per-booking maximum M and a total per-slot capacity C. The buyer explicitly confirms both; existing v1/v2 agreements remain unchanged. In the bounded supported range (M2–12, C3–12, M ≤ C < 2M), two different keys race for the same slot, the winning request is replayed without consuming more capacity, the remainder fills the slot, one additional guest is rejected, and another slot plus the original receipts are read back. A fresh local native-SQLite/loopback-HTTP exercise kept the buyer’s M6/C8 unchanged: the fixed handler supported17/17; an overbooking handler actually stored10 guests and produced3 mismatches with8 unavailable steps; a native capacity of7 caused1 mismatch with4 unavailable steps. These new v3 observations are local controlled tests, not a new hosted or third-party deployment. A read-only connection preflight and a browser offline-evidence viewer also reduce setup and review friction. See the [2026-11-03 depth verification record](docs/DEPTH-VERIFICATION-2026-11-03.md) for exact scope and remaining evidence gaps.

The new optional **combined 31-step booking protocol**, `booking-http-v4`, preserves the two original party/capacity clauses and adds a separately confirmed wider technical recipe. It keeps v3's 17 capacity steps and adds 14: six invalid-input probes (missing time, malformed email, zero/fractional/negative/string party sizes), one winning-key conflict, three same-key concurrency/readback steps, and four one-guest/maximum−1 creation/readback steps. Fresh capacity examples propose v4; v3 remains selectable, and existing v1/v2/v3 agreements and reports retain their original 8/18/17 steps. A seven-family scope disclosure derives planned, observed, mismatched and unavailable counts from saved named steps; pending or unrun checks do not borrow old counts. These are booking-protocol counts, not a website coverage percentage or AI accuracy measure.

A controlled native-SQLite/owned-loopback test demonstrates the added coverage: a deliberately permissive email handler still supports the shorter v3 17/17 valid-email baseline, but v4 actually stores one fictional malformed-email booking, then stops its dependent capacity path with 6 supported, 2 mismatched and 23 unavailable steps after 8 HTTP requests. The corrected handler supports 31/31 in that bounded run and retains six distinct synthetic receipts. No new hosted result follows from these local tests. The append-only standalone `0002_capacity_expanded.sql` and hosted-fixture `0005_capacity_expanded.sql` add separate expanded state; existing migrations and old report meanings are preserved. See [coverage expansion and the exact evidence limits](docs/COVERAGE-EXPANSION-2026-11-03.md).

Seller-owned staging is now supported through a bounded, project-scoped operator registry. The seller can download the locked HTTP contract, implement it against an authorised staging handler and datastore, and generate a local registration proposal from the exact static export. After operator review, the server checks the approved project, protocol, uploaded-file digest, expiry and full declaration before each new run. The target must echo the matching declaration metadata before any booking writes occur. Delivery, reports and explicit acceptance preserve the same declaration; it is an association, not deployed-code attestation. Removed or expired approval cannot strand an expired persisted lease: members may close that original run with zero-network unavailable evidence, preserving its unknown external outcome. See [the seller staging guide](docs/OWN-STAGING-BACKEND.md) and [the dated comparison and test record](docs/WINNER-ITERATIONS-2026-11-02.md). No independent third-party seller deployment has yet been demonstrated.

## Optional contracts and per-criterion AI assessment

A contract is optional. The default intake lets the buyer type website requirements, enter the price and confirm a manual checklist without adding any document or calling AI. An explicit “Organize these requirements” button can suggest a checklist from that description. The collapsed optional agreement section accepts pasted text or UTF-8 TXT; PDF, Word and OCR are not implemented. A direct-description edit invalidates its adopted AI draft; document-derived sources remain separate from the project description.

Every new custom project also accounts for the original website summary without requiring AI or a contract. Literal punctuation-based groups retain exact source text: include each as a separate human acceptance check, explicitly exclude it with a reason, or leave it pending clarification and block creation. A compound group must be checked in full; grouping does not claim semantic understanding. The server regenerates the source groups, requires the exact current source text and all decisions, and saves the confirmed scope. Editing the source or proposed checks invalidates confirmation. Saved projects show the full original and excluded requirements/reasons. Earlier agreements are not rewritten or given invented historical coverage.

After seller files are saved and the current version has a complete rule review, either participant can explicitly request an independent DeepSeek source assessment. It compares the locked criteria with saved HTML/CSS, with no original contract required. New requests send the complete confirmed scope summary, agreed criteria, all validated HTML/CSS text, rule findings and metadata for every delivered file. The original contract, customer identity, amount and older versions are excluded. Raster files are listed as omitted from model-source coverage: they can appear in the protected preview, but this text-only assessment does not inspect their pixels or judge photo quality. A supplied image excluded from model input is not a missing delivery asset. No key means a visible unconfigured error, never a fake AI result. Role-play rule review and payment remain demo, while this separately disclosed action can use the configured live model without changing those modes.

Each criterion gets `supported`, `changes_suggested` or `insufficient_evidence`, a bounded explanation and exact source citations. The server derives UTF-16 citation offsets only from text actually included in the model request. It checks complete unique criterion coverage and rejects invented citations or extra fields. Server rule failures take priority; browser/backend behavior, mobile usability, subjective appearance and unclassified manual criteria stay insufficient. Literal content checks such as opening hours and menu text may receive source-based suggestions, subject to these exclusions. This report never changes rule evidence, human checks, acceptance or financial state.

The active V3 protocol selects enumerated IDs from a frozen catalog of original UTF-16 source spans; the server restores the exact occurrence rather than accepting rewritten model quotations. Complete admitted HTML/CSS files are sent without truncation, and bounded file/owner witnesses check cross-field relationships separately from ID validity. New V3 reports publish actual inventory and execution-boundary notes instead of unverified model limitation prose. If rule/evidence handling changes any model item status, all optional model risks are temporarily withheld with an explicit count; this can also withhold unrelated useful leads and does not mean those issues were resolved. See [the citation and publication protocol](docs/ASSESSMENT-CITATION-PROTOCOL.md).

A narrowly recognized, explicit consultation/booking-policy **copy** instruction can retain its cited model verdict, including an insufficient or suggested-change verdict. This is a bounded wording allowance, not a general natural-language intent classifier: unfamiliar policy phrasing may still need manual confirmation. Extra operational clauses, an operational title, or mixed booking/form/backend behavior do not receive this allowance. Invented citations are rejected. Historical reports retain their original coverage and conservative handling of incomplete HTML; opening them does not substitute new source or rerun the model. No text verdict proves that a reservation service works.

The assessment may also list up to five cited **potential** source problems. It does not run scripts, a browser or a backend, and does not establish that a site is secure or bug-free. New requests include all text allowed by the upload limits, without silently clipping file prefixes or removing comments; a serialized request above 850,000 UTF-8 bytes or the conservative context admission limit fails before contacting the provider. New coverage records therefore include every HTML/CSS file in full, have no truncated text files, and exclude raster pixels. Historical included/truncated/omitted coverage remains readable, with omitted text distinguished from excluded image pixels. Only an explicit request calls the model, at most once per saved report; opening the saved report is free of new calls. Concurrent first requests may each reach the provider before the database CAS allows only one saved result. There is no automatic retry. The completion allowance ranges from 1,800 to 6,400 tokens according to criterion count, with thinking disabled; the four new complete-source cases each used 1,800.

Either project participant can append a human review of one potential risk: **Issue confirmed**, **Finding rejected** or **More evidence needed**, with a required reason. Every entry records the actor role, time, exact delivery ID, assessment ID and risk index. A derived summary counts each role's latest confirmed/rejected/needs-evidence positions, unreviewed findings and differing positions separately. Reviewed does not mean resolved, and one participant's position does not erase the other's. Complete history remains readable; another annotation appends to that history rather than overwriting an earlier disagreement. Identical repeated input is idempotent. These are participant statements, not independent security verification. The raw AI report, citations and original risk text remain unchanged, and an annotation cannot change rule evidence, buyer acceptance or payment state. Replacement versions do not inherit current risk reviews; immutable history keeps the earlier version's records.

Reports are stored in `delivery.assessment`, private to project participants, and omitted from project-list summaries. Replacements discard any report on the new delivery; immutable history preserves the old report with its original delivery. UI and endpoint checks bind assessment to the current delivery ID and explicit send confirmation. Existing buyer acceptance remains independent and can be completed without running optional AI assessment.

## From requirements to a confirmed website checklist

In **My project**, paste the original contract or requirements, or upload one UTF-8 `.txt` file. Inputs support up to 12,000 UTF-16 characters and 48 KiB of UTF-8 text. This version does not parse PDF, Word documents, scanned images or OCR. Adding a contract or using extraction is optional. It prioritizes static HTML/CSS website delivery rather than a general marketplace or an automatic legal review.

Without `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY`, the interface labels the result **rule-based extraction — no AI request**. Rules suggest only explicit root-level HTML filenames and a fully stated local-link source, quoted label and `page.html#id` target. They do not invent filenames from words such as “home page” or invent a booking anchor. Other requirements remain human checks or unresolved clauses. With a configured model key, extraction sends the original text to DeepSeek and validates the JSON response, supported fields and exact source quotations. A provider error or invalid output is shown as a failure, never disguised as a successful AI result or silently replaced with a rule result.

The buyer applies the suggestions to an editable checklist, checks the complete original and unresolved items, adds or changes checks, and explicitly confirms that final list before creating the project. The project amount and currency are entered and confirmed separately; extraction cannot approve a price, accept delivery or initiate payment. Scope approval then locks the final project criteria and amount.

Every retained extraction citation has an exact `start`, `end` and `excerpt` matching the original text, with JavaScript UTF-16 offsets. Only final page, link and manual-check fields that still exactly match a saved suggestion inherit its source reference. A buyer's edited or newly added field does not inherit a misleading extracted citation. System-suggested technical and mobile checks are distinguished from original agreement wording. Both the full original and unresolved clauses stay available for inspection alongside the final checklist.

`POST /api/requirements` requires platform-authenticated identity and stores a server-generated draft in D1, private to its creator. A draft can be used for seven days; retrieval checks its owner and database timestamp. Expiry prevents using the draft to create another project; it is not a claim that the stored row has been automatically deleted. When the buyer confirms and creates a project, its own copy of the original and confirmed references is saved with that project and follows project-participant access. Project lists omit the original contract text. Extraction alone creates no payment project or order.

## Buyer and seller access

The creator of a collaborative project is its buyer. After approving and locking the scope, the buyer can generate a seven-day invitation bound to the seller's authenticated sign-in email. The seller must be a different account, inspect the agreement, confirm its scope and join explicitly. The server stores the invitation's SHA-256 hash rather than its bearer token; accepting, revoking or replacing the invitation invalidates the old link. Membership and action permissions are checked on the server using platform-provided identity, not a role selector or a client-supplied user ID.

The buyer shares the invitation link manually. The application does not send email or grant website access. Invitations carry the token in the URL fragment and send it in POST bodies for inspection and acceptance; successful joining clears the fragment before opening the project. If sign-in loses the fragment, reopen the original invitation after signing in. An owner-private Site still requires the owner to grant the seller website access separately. Joining a project does not change the Site's audience or access policy.

In the default custom two-sided demo, switch to Seller to upload your own files; this mode cannot borrow the fixed built-in sample's deliverables. The [bilingual noodle-shop guide](docs/NOODLE-NAVIGATION-DEMO.md) supplies a four-page, three-link flawed/revised exercise. In a local UI run, v1 saved twelve observed/two unverified steps, while v2 saved fourteen observed/zero issues/zero unverified; a separate eight-reading layout run had no overflow or unavailable result. These are developer-operated fixture results, not independent user research; the [follow-up record](docs/DELIVERY-COVERAGE-2026-11-02.md) separates the API, model and browser evidence.

The seller can select a website folder while preserving bounded relative directories, or select supported files. A delivery contains at most **40 HTML/CSS/PNG/JPEG/WebP files**, with root-level `index.html`. Each HTML/CSS file is at most **64 KiB** and text totals at most **256 KiB**, measured as UTF-8 bytes. Each raster image is at most **256 KiB** and raster bytes total at most **1 MiB**, measured before storage encoding. Canonical relative paths allow at most five ASCII filename segments and 160 characters; traversal, aliases, conflicting paths and case collisions are rejected. Raster structures and dimensions are checked; GIF, animated WebP/APNG, SVG, fonts and JavaScript are outside this upload format. The server creates an independent exact snapshot and delivery ID. Other missing required pages are saved with failed findings so the seller can inspect and replace them. Every replacement is a complete file set; it does not merge with the previous version. Replacement archives its predecessor and clears the current review and acceptance. In the optional single-user mode, the creator performs the upload instead.

Custom files can be uploaded, previewed and reviewed **before payment authorization**, after locking the scope and, for collaboration, joining as seller. URLs and ZIP archives are not upload inputs; unpack a ZIP first and choose its website folder. This is a bounded static-file preview, not deployment of a JavaScript application. The protected preview serves supported saved CSS and raster assets from that delivery, including relative directory references; scripts, form submissions and external subresources do not run. Source resource checks separately record missing local targets, references outside the supported saved-resource scope, and uninspectable syntax. A `blocked` external-reference finding means the source reference is outside that scope; it is not evidence that a browser navigation was attempted and blocked. Links can still navigate the preview away from the saved files; select a saved entry to return and confirm acceptance only against that delivery.

The conservative HTML checker looks for independent documents with a doctype, nonempty title and body, and unique source links/targets for the agreed descriptors. Ambiguous markup fails the check; optional closing tags and unsupported entities can fail even when a browser would repair or display them. A source-document finding does not prove that the page's content, design or behavior meets the brief. Mobile usability and every free-text requirement always need direct human confirmation.

The optional layout check observes saved pages in the current browser at 375 px and 900 px and stores a report bound to the delivery ID. These are client-reported browser observations, not independent server measurements. Missing or unavailable readings do not count as clean results. Neither a clean width report nor an AI explanation automatically passes mobile usability or replaces the buyer's manual checks.

The separate **Link behavior & resource checks** action observes the saved version at the same two widths. It verifies saved text responses, programmatically activates only the agreed local links, and records link visibility, an uncovered sample point, the expected destination and target visibility. It also checks applicable saved stylesheets and preloads/decodes page images. Wrong destinations and unsupported or changed navigation remain unverified instead of counting as matches. The server derives the plan, outcome summary and reporter role from saved scope and membership, validates complete step coverage, and uses the previous report ID for optimistic concurrency. Authorized participants may append observations to an accepted or paid current version when no payment operation is pending; the action cannot alter human checks, acceptance, consent or payment. Each genuinely saved run is retained on that delivery, while the latest-report field remains available for current display. Replacement versions start without inherited observations; authorized version history retains the old reports. These are bounded client observations, not full keyboard/touch/accessibility tests, image-meaning analysis, CSS-background-image checks, backend tests or security verification. Actual positive and seeded-failure browser results are recorded in the [functional verification record](docs/FUNCTIONAL-VERIFICATION-2026-11-02.md).

**Check history & acceptance evidence** lists the saved browser/layout runs for the exact delivery. A new explicit acceptance stores an immutable snapshot of criteria, evidence, matching review/AI/risk annotations and human checks, with references to the browser/layout reports that existed then or explicit absence. A later report is a supplement: it does not replace the reports bound to the earlier acceptance. Older acceptances without a snapshot remain labelled unavailable; only the genuinely saved latest legacy observation can be retained on the first upgrade write. Each observation type allows up to 100 reports per delivery, acceptance evidence up to 20 snapshots, and the total evidence history is bounded to 256 KiB. A full history rejects a new save without silently removing older records. See the [coverage and evidence follow-up](docs/DELIVERY-COVERAGE-2026-11-02.md) for the implementation boundary and [noodle navigation exercise](docs/NOODLE-NAVIGATION-DEMO.md) for report A → acceptance → report B steps.

**Compare each requirement** opens a compact, criterion-level comparison between an older immutable snapshot and the current delivery. It preserves the exact agreed clause, shows buyer feedback matched to the older delivery and requirement, and presents each version's rule excerpts, source-AI statuses/citations and browser readings separately. Records with another delivery ID, missing reports and uncovered requirements are explicitly unavailable; current evidence is never substituted into the old column. Width readings are file-level context, not a browser verdict on a text requirement. A newer sequence or smaller aggregate problem count cannot establish that an issue is fixed. Comparison is read-only and neither accepts work nor initiates payment.

## How ProofBeforePay works

1. The buyer optionally extracts a draft from pasted requirements or UTF-8 TXT, reviews exact source quotations and unresolved clauses, edits and explicitly confirms the checklist, and enters the amount separately. Choose real collaboration to invite a different seller account; the default custom two-sided demo instead lets the creator experience both perspectives without another account. Approve the exact scope and amount. For real collaboration, generate and manually share an email-bound seller invitation and arrange website access separately.
2. The invited seller inspects and confirms the agreement, joins using the matching authenticated account, then uploads the complete supported website folder with HTML/CSS and local PNG/JPEG/WebP assets. Buyer and seller may inspect the current files and run source/layout checks. Custom projects cannot borrow the built-in demonstration's files or findings.
3. The seller reviews the current version, writes a delivery note and submits that version for buyer acceptance. The note is a seller statement, separate from system findings. After submission the seller cannot replace the files unless the buyer returns the delivery for changes.
4. The buyer previews saved pages, checks each criterion and can return specific comments. Rule explanations are provider-free; separately requested DeepSeek source assessment retains its own cited advice. Either participant may append a reasoned human review of a potential AI risk without editing the model report. Neither the model nor the seller's note decides acceptance or money movement.
5. The seller addresses feedback and submits a complete revision. Replacement archives old files, review, source assessment, human risk annotations, submission note and version-bound feedback atomically with the current-version update. Both participants can read earlier versions and compare the same criterion's records. Opening history does not replace the active delivery; each revision needs a fresh review and submission.
6. The buyer completes every required human check and explicitly accepts the **current submitted delivery ID**. This records `collaboration.acceptance` only: it does not create a payment order, authorize funds, capture a payment or reuse that acceptance as financial consent.
7. If the buyer continues, ordinary collaboration uses separate simulated authorization and capture actions. The explicitly selected new two-account Sandbox test choice uses PayPal's test merchant instead. Only the buyer may act, and capture still requires an active authorization, exact-version acceptance, required human checks and separate financial confirmation. Neither path settles money to the actual seller. A dated two-account hosted test completed the latter path with AUD 1 in virtual funds; it is not evidence of independent first-use testing.

The optional single-user custom mode retains creator upload/review before Sandbox authorization. Custom `mode: "custom", demoRoleplay: true` cases use the buyer-confirmed custom scope and actual uploaded files, with provider-free delivery review and simulated payment. Built-in `mode: "demo", demoRoleplay: true` cases retain their separate fixed sample setup and controlled files. Existing older built-in cases retain their authorization-first flow, and existing BriefPay quotes keep their original checkout path. These modes do not gain a real seller account or an invitation automatically.

The following Sandbox behavior applies to the preserved single-user path and explicit new Sandbox test modes, not ordinary simulated collaboration. Before contacting PayPal, the server records financial consent and freezes the exact delivery while capture is pending. Verify the saved order, authorization, capture status, currency and exact amount before recording a paid receipt. Capture/void retries use stable request identifiers and provider-state checks to reconcile uncertain outcomes.

When a Sandbox capture or void is pending, **Check PayPal status** performs a read-only reconciliation of the saved order, authorization and capture resources. A matching completed capture can produce a receipt only when the previously frozen acceptance matches the current delivery. A verified void closes the authorization; a verified denied authorization with no capture closes the agreement as **closed, unpaid**. These outcomes are saved with the same optimistic concurrency check as other updates.

Pending payments, active authorizations, rejected/failed captures, unavailable responses and inconsistent resource histories keep the original operation and accepted delivery frozen. A failed capture alone does not prove that the authorization is closed. This prototype does not automatically clear those locks or start a replacement charge; review the Sandbox transaction records or obtain provider support if repeated queries remain unresolved. The local 24-hour acceptance window is never used as evidence that PayPal's authorization expired. The status lookup itself does not initiate capture or void.

If the buyer declines an uncaptured delivery, explicit cancellation **voids the authorization**. Void is different from refund: refund applies after a payment has been captured. A pending capture must be reconciled before a cancellation can proceed. Closing a browser tab or abandoning the page is not a confirmed cancellation.

Buyer and seller permissions come from their saved membership, and the server enforces each workflow stage. The buyer cannot upload a collaborative delivery; the seller cannot accept it or perform payment operations. Every delivery upload or sample replacement must include `expectedDeliveryId` (`null` for the first delivery, otherwise the exact version shown to the uploader). A missing or stale baseline returns 409 without replacing files; the interface preserves the file selection and refreshes the case for review. Replacement retires the old `/deliveries/...` current-preview URL, while immutable files remain accessible through the authorized version API and `/proof-versions/...` history preview. Proof-case and version list endpoints omit full file contents. Completed or pending payment cases cannot replace delivery files. The prototype does not provide seller settlement, automatic visual/usability acceptance, refunds after capture or a production dispute service.

## 给初学者的解释

把它理解成三层：网页让你确认约定、查看证据和做验收决定；后端核对交付版本、调用模型并处理付款；数据库记住每个决定与支付状态。

首次进入先看到“约定 11–8 点→初版写成 10–6 点→查看证据→修订后人工确认”的具体示意。示意不冒充已经运行的结果；点击“体验交付与修改流程”才创建虚构样板，也可以直接填写自己的需求，返回介绍时保留当前页面内的草稿。

新建“我的项目”会保留网站需求原文，即使不调用 AI、不添加合同，也要逐条选择纳入人工验收、待澄清或明确排除。纳入条目单独要求人工确认；排除要写理由；待澄清不能创建。分组基于标点，不代表自动理解语义，复合条目需要全部检查。新验收会记录当时证据快照，之后重复检查只追加记录；旧验收缺少快照时不会事后补造。可用[双语面馆练习](docs/NOODLE-NAVIGATION-DEMO.md)核对四页、三条静态导航及验收前后报告；不包含真实预约或邮件后台。

“我的项目”现在先做网页交付：买家粘贴合同／需求，或导入 UTF-8 TXT，系统整理验收清单。当前没有密钥时用规则，不调用 AI；配置后可由 DeepSeek 提供建议。暂不支持 PDF、Word、扫描件或 OCR。买家需要对照完整原文和未明确项，编辑、确认清单，并单独填写金额。每条真实提取来源都能回到原文；改写或新增的标准不会冒充原合同条款。未创建项目的草稿只属于创建者，七天内可使用；确认后的原文和标准随项目保存。

使用方式默认是“双端体验 · 模拟付款”：用自己的需求和文件，在同一账号下切换买家与卖家，不创建真实对方账号；可选 AI 评估另行运行。卖家可选择保留目录的网站文件夹，包含 HTML/CSS 和本地 PNG/JPEG/WebP 照片。最多 40 文件，文本单个 64 KiB、合计 256 KiB，图片单个 256 KiB、合计 1 MiB，根目录包含 index.html；脚本、表单和外部子资源不运行。与别人协作时，分别可选“邀请真实卖家 · 模拟付款”或新建时明确选择“邀请真实卖家 + Sandbox 虚拟支付”。创建者锁定要求后生成绑定卖家邮箱的七天邀请，自行分享；邀请不发邮件、不授予网站访问权限。卖家用受邀账号确认约定、交付并提交；买家按标准退回意见，卖家再上传整份修订文件，旧版证据和反馈保留。

源码检查能确认保守 HTML 结构、本地链接及资源引用，不能证明页面内容、设计或手机体验符合要求；“外部引用受限”不是浏览器已实际拦截一次导航的证明。逐项版本对照保留相同原始要求、旧版反馈和两版独立证据，缺失报告明确不可用，不按版本号判断已修复。双方可对 AI 潜在问题追加“确认／驳回／仍需证据”的理由，原始 AI 报告不改，也不代表验收。浏览器报告是客户端观测，不是服务器独立验证。买家完成必须的人工作业后，单独接受当前交付；这一步不付款。默认协作付款模拟；新建时可明确选择同一账号的 Sandbox 演练，或两个网站账号的 Sandbox 测试，均只向配置的测试商户虚拟付款，不向实际卖家结算。本轮两个独立认证账号已完成 v1 退回、v2 修订、两次真实 AI 评估、人工验收及单独授权／扣款 AUD 1 虚拟资金，两端刷新后仍显示已扣款，收据绑定验收的 v2。这两次登录由同一开发者通过自动化代理操作，不算两名独立真人测试。此前单账号 AUD 480 演练仍单独保留；三人首次使用测试和新连续录屏仍待进行。

新 AI 请求会发送上传范围内完整的 HTML/CSS，超出请求上限就明确失败，不再默默只截取文件前半段；照片像素不发送，旧报告仍按当时的覆盖范围显示。新浏览器功能会在 375／900px 尝试约定的本地链接、查看目标是否可见，并记录样式加载和页面图片解码；这仍不包含脚本、表单后台、邮件送达、完整可用性或安全性验证。人工已确认而 AI 无法独立判断时，两种状态会并列保留；AI 风险按买卖双方各自的最新意见汇总，不能把“写过意见”当作“问题已解决”。本轮实际检验与尚未完成项见[功能验证记录](docs/FUNCTIONAL-VERIFICATION-2026-11-02.md)。

看懂流程后，阅读 `lib/requirement-extraction.ts`（从原文到待确认清单）、`lib/requirement-store.ts`（私有草稿）、`lib/static-project.ts`（真实输入与证据）、`lib/proof-workflow.ts`（角色与交付阶段）、`lib/proof-store.ts`（成员、邀请与历史）、`lib/proof-domain.ts`（什么时候允许扣款）和 `lib/providers.ts`（外部接口）。这一版使用 TypeScript；HTTP、JSON、数据库和状态转换同样适用于后续学习 Python。
