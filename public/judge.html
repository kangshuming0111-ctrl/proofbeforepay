The demo needs no account and runs fixed-example checks in your browser. It makes no new AI call, acceptance or payment. The workbench is where state changes; project actions there require an authorized account.

The same flow, in the workbench

  1. Open a delivery. Choose “Try the noodle-shop delivery.” Its first website and rule checks are ready. Creating the example runs no AI.
  2. Inspect with AI, if configured. Before acceptance, expand “More checks,” choose “Open AI checks,” then “Assess current delivery with DeepSeek.” Opening the panel makes no model call. Check its advice and citations against the agreed brief.
  3. Revise and recheck. Request changes, switch to the fictional seller and send the prepared revision. As buyer, inspect and separately assess this version. Under “More checks,” open “Compare both versions’ evidence”; missing reports do not prove a repair.
  4. Accept. Confirm the manual checks and accept that exact version. Its evidence is frozen.
  5. Pay separately. Check the provider label. Demo payments are simulated; configured Sandbox payments use test funds.

This is one-account role play. If AI is unavailable, use rule checks and manual review. The dated AI evidence below is not a report for this project.
