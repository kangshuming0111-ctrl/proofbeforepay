One flow to try

  1. Agree. Open “Try a complete example” and confirm the fictional requirements.
  2. Deliver. Switch to the example’s seller role, save the flawed sample and submit it.
  3. Inspect and revise. Review the website and separate rule, AI/source and human findings. Request a revision, then submit the corrected version.
  4. Accept. Confirm the manual checks and accept that exact version. Its evidence is frozen.
  5. Pay separately. Check the provider label. Demo payments are simulated; configured Sandbox payments use test funds.

This built-in example is one-account role play. It does not prove independent buyer/seller participation.
