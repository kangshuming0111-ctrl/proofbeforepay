## What the local controls establish

The controlled role exercise used 15 retained reports: 24 published and six withheld leads across buyer/seller roles. It saved **60 local domain annotations**, checked **60 idempotent repeats**, rejected **68 wrong/absent-pool requests** and **120 stale delivery/report requests**. These are local in-memory domain operations, not database persistence, real membership authentication, independent human research or a new hosted test.

The final post-scope suite recorded **1,478/1,478 passing tests** with no failures. This is the retained local test result; it does not substitute for full-answer semantic review, broad runtime coverage, deployment verification or an award outcome.
